FerriSync is a private, peer-to-peer file synchronization app for your local network.

No cloud. No account. No central server. Your devices discover each other on the LAN, pair with explicit consent, and sync folders directly over encrypted connections (TLS 1.3, trust-on-first-use).

Features:
- Discover and pair devices on the same local network
- Bidirectional folder synchronization
- Conflict handling with backup copies
- QR code pairing
- Foreground service to keep sync running in the background

Permissions:
- All files access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): required to sync arbitrary folders you explicitly choose. FerriSync only reads/writes the folders you add for synchronization.
- Foreground service and notifications: keep synchronization active and show sync status while running in the background.
- Camera: optional QR code pairing.
- Internet/local network: direct device-to-device sync on your LAN.

Status: developer preview. Behavior and UI may change.
