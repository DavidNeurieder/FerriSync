FerriSync 0.1.0 — first developer preview.

- Discover and pair devices on your local network, with consent required for new peers
- Sync folders both ways over encrypted TLS 1.3 connections
- Keep backup copies of conflicting edits instead of silently overwriting them
- Optional QR code pairing and a foreground service for background sync

Known limitation: file deletions are not propagated between devices yet.
